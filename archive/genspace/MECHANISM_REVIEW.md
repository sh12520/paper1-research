# Public mechanism and label review — engineering only

This review checks the declared abstract model and guarded schedules, not the negotiation outcome or the research hypothesis. No SMOKE, PILOT or CONFIRM seed is used. `tests/test_mechanism_review.py` and `tests/test_runner_semantic.py` provide the executable small-domain checks; they do not establish aggregate agreement rates or the final engineering gates.

- **F1 CONDITIONAL_QOS.** In the configured positive pressure/jitter ranges, moving-state RAN capacity is 0.68 times still-state RAN capacity for each time. In a low-pressure rich ENGINEERING fixture, quality 0.8 fits the still-state RAN capacity but not moving, while quality 0.4 fits moving. A state-conditioned guarded profile is labeled `STATE_CONDITIONAL` by the independent cell-level classifier.
- **F2 DELAYED_START.** The time-zero RAN multiplier is 0.35 and later slots use multiplier 1. In the checked low-pressure fixture, time-zero capacity is below 0.4 and later capacity is at least 0.8. A one-slot delayed profile is labeled `DELAYED_START`. This demonstrates the specified release mechanism; it is not evidence that either agent accepts the contract.
- **F3 PHASED_SERVICE.** Time-zero RAN uses multiplier 0.65 and later slots multiplier 1. In the checked low-pressure fixture, quality 0.4 fits the first slot, 0.8 does not, and 0.8 fits the next slot. A first-low/then-high guarded schedule receives `TEMPORAL_PHASED`. The test checks the stated mechanism, not that it is feasible in every pressure configuration.
- **F4 EDGE_OFFLOAD.** For every positive configured quality and rate, offload reduces base RAN demand from q to 0.5q and FH demand from 0.4r to 0.28r, while adding base EDGE demand 0.5q. The enabled offload role also adds 0.1 EDGE per active cell in the current registry. The checked low-pressure fixture has a high-quality unoffloaded RAN demand above capacity, while offloaded RAN, FH and surcharge-inclusive EDGE fit. This is an abstract exchange, not a measured network effect.

`mechanism_labels` derives labels from all guarded cell values, not the lexical AST witness. `STATIC` means all cells are active with an identical full non-offloaded value; `STATE_CONDITIONAL` means at least one time has different state-cell values, including active-role and cost differences; `DELAYED_START` means a state is initially idle but later active; `TEMPORAL_PHASED` means two adjacent active cells differ; `OFFLOAD` means at least one active offloaded cell; and `HYBRID` means at least two of the four non-static mechanisms. Idle-to-active alone is not counted as phased. Structural novelty remains the separate exact semantic non-membership test against B2, so `STATIC` is a behavioral label rather than an assertion of B2 membership.

The independent test classifier is applied to all 562 ASTs in a two-profile depth-two public grammar and checks equal semantic keys have equal labels. The redundant `If(moving,P,P)` case is explicitly checked to have the B2 key and `STATIC` label. In addition, the final-snapshot graph comparison examined all 1,394,064 depth-two semantic records for each of four F1–F4 ENGINEERING fixtures: FAST labels matched a separately implemented packed-cell classifier for every record. Full per-candidate results are in `results/engineering/conditioned_fast_depth2_numeric_final.json`; the separate conditioned depth-one comparison used direct AST interpretation. The depth-two numeric report intentionally leaves lexical-witness validation pending; independent capability-conditioned syntax streams and final-snapshot rechecks provide that separate evidence, as bound in `results/engineering/conditioned_final_differential_provenance_v2.json`. Label equivalence is scoped to the audited fixed graph, encoded cells, tested snapshot and public model basis. The depth-two numeric comparison uses a packed reference, with its AST correspondence and limits described in `PACKED_REFERENCE_SCOPE.md`.
