# PILOT mechanism attribution

Post-PILOT exploratory same-instance attribution; not confirmation evidence. No Gate A–E determination, bootstrap or significance test is made. Subsets mean exact normalized semantic spaces, not selected-representative label filters. Rates in the mechanism matrix use each family’s B2-deadlock/full-O1-agreement denominator.

**Exploratory assessment: OFFLOAD_DOMINANT.** OFFLOAD-only is sufficient for 783/842 resolved B2 deadlocks, while 40/842 require two mechanism groups. The multi-group cases are real same-instance benchmark evidence, but all 40 require OFFLOAD together with another group; they do not establish a broadly distributed non-offload composition effect. Human review of claim scope and the existing PILOT distribution is recommended before deciding on CONFIRM freeze review. No parameter or gate was changed.

The exact filter uses the normalized semantic signature's whole-contract capability footprint: every constructor has an exclusive required role, and P covers both Sequence and ValidFor. Its class masks are intersected with the original instance's availability and depth masks. This is a semantic-set test, not filtering by selected witness or behavior labels. Full 27-profile depth-1 enumeration and reduced four-profile depth-2 syntax enumeration independently matched all 16 filtered sets; full depth-2 exactness additionally relies on the existing complete rich-graph audit and the documented footprint argument. Restricted representatives maximize Nash and are independently verified; equal-Nash ties compare stored normalized class witnesses, not all possible syntax witnesses. See `PILOT_ATTRIBUTION_INTEGRITY.md` and `results/pilot_attribution_work/INDEPENDENT_SPACE_AUDIT.md`.

Among the 842 target instances, 802 have at least one sufficient singleton group; 119 have a sufficient non-offload singleton; 100 have both OFFLOAD-only and a non-offload singleton. These categories overlap. The remaining 40 require multiple groups.
