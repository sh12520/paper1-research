# Descriptive statistics and boundaries

- Unit: one previously serialized PILOT instance; all 16 restricted spaces are evaluated on that same instance.
- Population: 1,920 instances; B2-deadlock/full-O1-agreement target: 842. Original depth-2 subset: 960.
- Among the 842 target instances: OFFLOAD-only sufficient 783, OFFLOAD necessary 721, no-OFFLOAD sufficient 121, minimum group cardinality 1/2/3/4 equal to 802/40/0/0.
- The 40 multi-group-necessary instances and the 40 depth-2-only instances are the same observed IDs. This overlap is empirical, not a theorem.
- The 16 restricted spaces are compared by exact class masks within the frozen abstract benchmark; each selected representative is independently checked. The full depth-2 space claim relies on the earlier complete graph audit and the normalized-footprint argument; independent syntax enumeration was exhaustive only for depth 1 at full profiles and a reduced depth-2 domain.
- No confidence interval, significance test, confirmation bootstrap, or Gate A–E decision was computed in this exploratory task, as requested. Counts are not evidence of generalization to other distributions or real networks.
