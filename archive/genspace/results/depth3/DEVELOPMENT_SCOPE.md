# DEPTH3-FEASIBILITY-01 scope and limits

The development grid comprises original PILOT seed-index 0, conflict high and configured depth 2, for every combination of four families, three pressures and two richness values. The runner binds 24 identities before any method execution. It copies each serialized record under a separate `__METHOD_D3` identity, records the source SHA256 and original instance, public and private hashes, and sets only the method's allowed depth to 3. It never opens the original outcome records online. The depth2 regression uses all 96 original seed-index-0 configurations at their original depths.

The depth3 structural stream includes every nonstatic normalized signature from the available grammar, with one through four mechanism groups. The earlier FLAT depth≤2 comparison retains its original one-or-two group filter. B2 begins the same bilateral protocol in the depth3 evaluation. The protocol can consume up to 5,000 verifier calls including its original batch and checkpoint boundaries. It uses the same public cursor as Top-K. Certified outputs are captured as the protocol consumes the stream, and the worker continues through K=500 when time remains. The query uses one budget for both activities. A no-agreement protocol result without domain exhaustion is recorded as unknown for existence.

Each query has 120 seconds of wall time, a sampled worker working-set ceiling of 2 GiB, 2,000,000 expansions and 260,000 pending or buffered frontier entries. The existing safety floors are 1,280 MiB available at launch and 768 MiB available during execution. A cooperative limit aborts output from an interrupted expansion. The cursor retains the interrupted predecessor's lower bound while its children or equality partitions are being installed. Expansion counts include both outer shape states, equality-partition probes and inner assignment refinements, recorded separately as well. Wall and memory checks are made at bounded loop operations and verifier batches. Peak worker working set is a query sample, not an interpolated hardware peak.

Top-K uses `(binary64 public score, unsigned lexicographic compact signature)`. The lower bound of every incomplete state must be strictly greater than an emitted candidate's score. The best buffered exact candidate must have strictly later `(score, signature)`. A domain with fewer than K candidates can certify its entire exact result when all frontiers are exhausted. Reports give shorter and full-length certificates separately.

The feasibility classification requires no soundness failures and a passing depth2 regression. `DEPTH3_FEASIBLE` also requires full-length K=500 certificates on at least 20 of 24 queries, including at least two pressures in each family-by-richness cell. `DEPTH3_PARTIAL` means at least one valid K=10 certificate and insufficient representative full-length K=500 coverage. `DEPTH3_INFEASIBLE` applies when soundness or regression fails or there are no valid K=10 certificates. This criterion is independent of new agreements.

The repository-root `DEPTH3_FEASIBILITY_REPORT.md` belongs to the earlier blocked gate and remains historical. The current report of the same name is stored in this new directory.
