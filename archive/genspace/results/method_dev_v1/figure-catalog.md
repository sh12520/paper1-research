# Development budget curves

- `figures/budget_orc.pdf`: ORC against the 842 oracle-recoverable B2 deadlocks.
- `figures/budget_nash_gap.pdf`: mean gap conditional on an agreement; consult summary.csv for varying denominators.
- `figures/budget_runtime.pdf`: median per-instance recorded runtime; shared graph loading is not included. FULL-O1 line uses its historical PILOT stage time, excludes B2 and is not directly comparable to development timings.

FULL-O1 is an unlimited offline reference. No confidence intervals or significance claims are shown.
