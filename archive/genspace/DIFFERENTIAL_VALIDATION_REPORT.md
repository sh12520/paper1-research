These checks use only `GENSPACE-FAIL-01-ENGINEERING` fixtures. They validate an implementation, not smoke, pilot or confirmation outcomes, and do not establish the research hypothesis. The final conditioned execution snapshot SHA-256 is `adef91e95c3f56eeb02319c83d52365d96c6703c70e3971ab2c8f12fba0fad7f`; graph SHA-256 is `11e84c86d15e58f64d8dcfc892e22a813cf1250c835a9d48fa96f22ea112f6fd`. `results/engineering/conditioned_fast_depth2_numeric_final.json` records every one of the **1,394,064** rich-graph classes for each of four F1–F4 ENGINEERING fixtures, with both rich and sparse settings; the capability-conditioned generated mask is checked separately. These are **validation coverage counts**, not agreement or research performance statistics. Missing classes, extra classes and lexical-witness mismatches were all zero; each applicable F1–F4 selected-winner result also matched, including the no-winner case. See `results/engineering/conditioned_reference_{00,19,26,31}/report.json`. The final-snapshot `snapshot_recheck.json` files recompare the retained original streams and inputs after explicitly allowlisted runner/reporting-only changes; they do not regenerate syntax. `results/engineering/conditioned_final_differential_provenance_v2.json` binds the original reports, rechecks, numeric report, boundary test and execution snapshot. The full depth-two reference here is the **packed evaluator**, not 5,576,256 separate AST verifier invocations. The separate full syntax-to-graph bytewise audit establishes graph semantic-set equality under its own recorded snapshot; it is not a substitute for a full direct AST execution comparison in these four fixtures.
